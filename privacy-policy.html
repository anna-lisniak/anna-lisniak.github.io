Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Anna Lisniak ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile applications for iPhone and iPad that we publish on the Apple App Store and that link to this Privacy Policy (each an "App" and together the "Apps"), and any related support communications (together with the Apps, the "Services"). The Apps do not send gameplay information to us or to anyone else.

Please read this Privacy Policy together with our Terms of Use. By downloading, installing, or using an App, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you are a parent or legal guardian, you accept this Privacy Policy on behalf of yourself and your child.

3. Our privacy commitments at a glance

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information from children.
  • We do not ask children to enter any information. The Apps have no text fields, chat, accounts, or profiles.
  • We do not show advertising of any kind, and we do not allow third parties to advertise in the Apps.
  • We do not use analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not use the camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, or location of your device.
  • We do not access the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and we do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information for money or for advertising.
  • Purchases are made through Apple. We never receive your name, Apple ID, or payment card details.

4. Information we do not collect

The Apps do not collect, and we do not receive from the Apps, any of the following:

  • names, nicknames, ages, birth dates, gender, photos, voice recordings, or other information about a child;
  • email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or other contact information;
  • device identifiers (such as the IDFA or IDFV), IP addresses, or precise or approximate location;
  • information about how a child plays, which activities they complete, or how long they play;
  • contacts, photos, files, or any other content stored on your device.

5. Information that stays on your device

To work properly, an App may keep a small amount of technical information locally on your device, for example game state or settings and the status of your purchases. This information:

  • is stored only on your device (and, where you have enabled it, in your device backups managed by Apple);
  • is not sent to us or to any third party;
  • does not identify you or your child; and
  • is deleted when you delete the App from your device (except for copies in backups you control).

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Some Apps offer free content and premium content that can be unlocked with a paid subscription or a one-time purchase (see our Terms of Use). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using Apple's in-app purchase system.

  • Apple collects and processes your payment details, billing information, and Apple ID under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive or store them.
  • To unlock premium content, the App uses Apple's StoreKit framework on your device to check whether a valid purchase or subscription exists. This check is performed between your device and Apple. We do not operate servers that receive this information.
  • Apple provides us with sales, subscription, and financial reports, such as the number of subscriptions, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and revenue by country or region. These reports are aggregated or pseudonymous and do not tell us who you are.
  • Subscriptions, cancellations, and refunds are managed in your Apple ID account settings. We cannot see or change your Apple ID account.

7. Information Apple may share with us

Apple may make certain information available to developers through App Store Connect, for example:

  • App Analytics: aggregated statistics such as the number of downloads, sessions, and deletions, and the countries where an App is used. Apple shares usage data with developers only from users who have agreed to share it in their device settings ("Share with App Developers").
  • Crash and performance reports: technical diagnostic reports, if you have agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers.
  • Ratings and reviews that you choose to post publicly on the App Store.

This information is provided to us by Apple in aggregated or de-identified form. We use it only to understand overall interest in the Apps, to find and fix technical problems, and to improve the Apps. We do not attempt to identify any individual from it. You can control what Apple shares in your device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements).

9. Children's privacy

Our Apps are designed for young children and are intended to be used by children under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

  • COPPA (United States). We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. Because the Apps do not collect personal information from children, they do not require verifiable parental consent for data collection.
  • GDPR and UK GDPR (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland). We do not process children's personal data through the Apps. We apply the principles of data minimization and privacy by design and by default.
  • Apple Kids Category. The Apps are designed to meet the App Store requirements for apps made for children: no third-party advertising, no third-party analytics, and no transmission of personally identifiable information or device information to third parties.
  • Parental gate. Purchases, links that leave an App, and other areas intended for adults are protected by a parental gate (a task that a young child is unlikely to complete), so that a parent or guardian is involved before any purchase or exit from the App.
  • No public sharing. Children cannot make any information public through the Apps.

If you believe that we have received personal information about a child, please contact us at lisniak.anna@gmail.com with the subject line "Child Privacy Request". We will promptly delete that information, except where we are required by law to keep it.
